The agency.

Two disciplines.
One considered output.

Duonox is two disciplines at one table: editorial instinct on one side, systems thinking on the other. The name works two ways, and so does the work.

We don't have a house look. We have a working method: gather the brief's actual material, find the structural idea that already lives inside it, and design from there. Most projects fall into three families: identity, editorial and print, and digital surfaces. The shapes change, but the rigour stays the same.

Our Approach

How considered work takes shape.

Four deliberate stages, with the same senior hands from the first call through launch and the months just after.

  1. Discovery

    We start by listening: goals, audience, and the real shape of the problem.

  2. Strategy

    A clear position and direction, agreed before a single pixel is drawn.

  3. Design & Build

    Identity, editorial, and digital surfaces, crafted in close tandem.

  4. Launch & Beyond

    We ship, then stay close for 90 days while the new system finds its feet.
